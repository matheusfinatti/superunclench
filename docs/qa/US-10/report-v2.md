## Notes
- **Tooling:** in keyboard mode, adb key events race the IME's focus moves (the hour auto-advances after a single digit). One attempt produced "1:00" instead of 6:15 ([05b](v2/05b-keyboard-input-partial-tooling.png)). I cancelled it and used the dial. A human typing is unaffected.
- The duration line's a11y label spells units out ("2 hours 30 minutes of quiet").
- Design §2/§2.1 matches: switch row, indented Starts/Ends rows with the primary-coloured times, "Next day" caption, duration line, picker title, mode-toggle icon bottom-left, Cancel/OK, red error line, disabled OK at reduced alpha. Light and dark both checked.
