## Observations
- **O1 (from US-04) explained.** The new "Last events" list shows a **Good at 20:30:44** (source `?`, recorded before this build) that promoted L2 2/3 → L3 0/4 during the 3 h idle, followed by 3 misses and auto-pause ([02](02-readout-last-events-legacy.png)). So a real answer was recorded, probably someone tapping the heads-up, not an engine bug.
- **O2: short intervals + auto-pause make hands-free QA hard.** With L1 = 5 s, three unanswered alarms auto-pause the session in about 15 s. This happened while I was on the Alarms & reminders screen ([09](09-auto-paused-after-3-misses.png), [10](10-readout-3-misses-replaced.png), and again in [32](32-auto-pause-after-3-misses-events.png)). It follows the US-07 rules, but a debug "disable auto-pause" toggle, or the AC's 1 min → 5 s scale, would help. In both cases the heads-up was still visible just after the auto-pause, and a tap on its **Good** pill recorded nothing. Engineer: check that the notification is cancelled when the session auto-pauses (it may just be the heads-up animating out).
- **O3: possible race at 5 s cadence.** The sequence `23:09:14 G card` then `23:09:19 M replaced` suggests the alarm scheduled before the card answer may still have fired about 5 s later and been counted as a miss. It could be a sub-second race between answer and alarm delivery. Low priority; worth a unit test that an answer cancels the pending AlarmManager alarm.
- Pause/Resume and the countdown row are not present yet (US-06). The chip shows **Paused** after auto-pause, with only Stop available.
