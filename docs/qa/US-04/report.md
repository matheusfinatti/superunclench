## Observation to investigate
- **O1: unexplained promotion during a 3 h idle.** I left the session **Running at L2 2/3**, History 16 (≈19:34, [38](38-readout-L2-2of3-running-before-idle.png)). At 22:44 the app showed **L3 0/4, Paused (auto), Misses 3, History 22** ([39](39-home-after-3h-idle-L3-paused.png), [40](40-readout-before-kill.png)).
  - Six events were recorded.
  - Auto-pause after 3 misses is expected (US-07 logic is already in `SessionEngine`), and misses are neutral in code.
  - Reaching L3 needs a **Good**. I did not answer anything in that window. Someone may have tapped Good on a heads-up; otherwise an answer or miss was misapplied.
  - Stats is still empty ("No check-ins yet", US-08), so I can't see the event sequence.
  - Engineer: please check the history log on the device, or add a history dump to the QA readout.
- **B3 from US-03** (first heads-up after the permission grant) was not re-tested. It needs a reinstall or a permission reset.

## Visual deviations from `docs/design/US-04-srs-progression.md`
1. **Level card:** matches §2. The "LEVEL" overline, the right-aligned `primary` level name, the large number with "of 8", the segmented bar (12dp, gaps, `primary`/`surfaceContainerHighest`, `tertiary` at max), the caption, the divider and the timer icon + interval line are all present.
2. **Feedback messages:** the exact copy and icons from §3/§5 in both light ([04](04-L2-level-up-message-light.png), [08](08-L3-bad-0of4-reset-message.png), [09](09-L3-0of4-bad-demote-L2.png), [10](10-L1-0of3-bad-floor-message.png), [21](21-L8-good-still-unclenched.png)) and dark ([24](24-L8-bad-demote-L7-dark.png), [27](27-L7-to-L8-level-up-dark.png)). Level-down is never red.
3. **Notification header has no "Level n · every …" sub-text** ([31](31-shade-notification-L2.png)), which differs from US-03 §4.2. This is intentional: `AndroidCheckInNotifier` says sub-text was dropped because it truncates the title on API 31+. The Designer should confirm and update §4.2.
4. **Launcher icon:** the calm face on light blue shows in the Recents app chip ([45](45-recents-new-app-icon.png), [46](46-recents-icon-chip-zoom.png)). I couldn't screenshot it on the home screen or in the app drawer (needs a swipe). The notification large icon still showed the old green Android robot at 19:32 ([31](31-shade-notification-L2.png)), probably because SystemUI caches icons after an incremental install. Re-check after a clean install.
5. The design's segment-drain animation was visible (07). The level-number slide and bar pulse happen too fast to verify with screenshots.
